# Angry Cats privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Angry Cats is an offline physics puzzle game operated by Fahmi Ahmed Ghazal / FamZz Games.

## Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account and does not collect or share personal information.
It has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases, social features or
user-generated content. The Android game does not request Internet permission.

Unlocked levels, stars, best scores and the sound preference are stored locally on your
device. They are not sent to FamZz Games or synchronized to a server. These records are
used only to provide the game and remember your progress and settings.

## Deleting local progress

You can reset saved progress from the Levels screen, under "Meet the crew & settings".
Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it also removes the game's local data. No
server-side game account or personal-data record is created by the game.

## Children

The game is intended for all ages, including children. The same local-only data handling
applies to every player. The game does not ask children for names, contact details,
location or other personal information, and does not include advertising or chat.

## Support communications

If you voluntarily email support, your email address and message will be handled to
answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or children's personal
information. Your email provider and the support email provider process those messages
under their own privacy policies.

## Distribution platforms

Google Play and your device's operating system may process information for installation,
updates, security and their own services under their respective privacy policies. This
does not cause the game to upload your local scores or saved progress to FamZz Games.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact **famzzpos@gmail.com**.

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
