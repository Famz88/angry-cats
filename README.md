## ✨ Features

- **100 Levels**: Procedurally designed fortresses ranging from simple wooden sheds to massive multi-story stone citadels.
- **4 Playable Cat Heroes**:
  - 🟧 **Orange Tabby**: Standard balanced heavy impact.
  - 🥷 **Ninja Cat**: Mid-air tap triggers a high-velocity dash forward.
  - 💣 **Chonky Bomb Coon**: Mid-air tap detonates a massive radial explosion.
  - 🔀 **Calico Splitter**: Mid-air tap splits into 3 fast mini-kittens mid-flight.
- **Interactive Camera Controls**:
  - 🔍 **Pinch-to-Zoom & Mouse Wheel Zoom** (0.35x to 2.5x).
  - 📜 **Drag to Pan/Scroll** anywhere across the battlefield.
  - 🎯 **Dynamic Flight Tracking** camera that follows airborne cats automatically.
- **Procedural BGM & Sound Effects**: Upbeat background music and synthesized impact/meow/explosion SFX using the Web Audio API (no external asset downloads required!).
- **TNT Hazards & Mouse Bosses**: Explosive TNT crates and crowned **Mouse Kings** with high health points.
- **Progressive Star Saving**: Automatically saves unlocked levels and high scores in your browser's `localStorage`.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Control (Desktop) | Control (Mobile) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aim & Launch Slingshot** | Click & Drag cat back, release | Touch & Drag cat back, release |
| **Activate Cat Ability** | Click screen while cat is in air | Tap screen while cat is in air |
| **Pan / Scroll View** | Click & Drag empty background area | Single finger swipe empty area |
| **Zoom In / Out** | Mouse Wheel / On-screen `+` & `-` | Pinch to Zoom / On-screen `+` & `-` |

- **HTML5 Canvas** — 2D graphics rendering
- **Matter.js** — 2D rigid-body physics simulation engine
- **Web Audio API** — Real-time synthesized music & audio effects
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Zero framework dependencies
